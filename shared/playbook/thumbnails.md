# Thumbnails

## Long-form

- 16:9, at least 1280 x 720, JPG or PNG, 2 MB or less (Blotato forwards a public `thumbnailUrl`; the channel must be phone-verified).
- At most 4 words, at most 3 focus areas, high contrast, readable at 160 px wide (the mobile feed). Design for a glance, not a read.
- Do not repeat the title; the thumbnail carries the image or the number, the title carries the words.
- Render 3 genuinely different variants from the spec's thumbnail concepts (`render-longform` renders stills). The reviewer can run Test & Compare in Studio (winner by watch-time share, about two weeks, desktop only, no API).
- Brand: dark background, amber accent `#FFB347`, warm white text `#F5F0E8`, one focal object.

## Shorts

- No custom thumbnail. 99.9 % of Shorts views come from the feed where no thumbnail shows. Frame 1 is the thumbnail: hook text fully legible, safe area respected.
