# Hashtags and Tags

## Hashtags

- Up to 3 show above the title; use 2-3 relevant ones, specific product names first (`#dgxspark #deepseek #localai`).
- More than 60 hashtags on one video and YouTube ignores all of them (the old "15" figure is stale). Stay at 2-3 anyway.
- No spaces inside a hashtag; every hashtag must relate to the content (misleading hashtags break the spam policy).
- `#Shorts` is optional and adds nothing.

## Tags

- Tags play a minimal role in discovery; they help with misspellings and variants only. Blotato does not send them, so the package note keeps a `tags` list for a future direct-API uploader and nothing depends on it.
- When present: 8-15 lowercase phrases, 500 characters total including commas, the exact primary keyword, 2-3 variants, common misspellings, the topic and the format. Nothing irrelevant.
