# Hook Library

Write 10 candidates, score them, keep two: one for each draft. The hook is sentence one of the narration and the text on frame 1; the package stage writes the title separately and must not reuse it.

| Pattern | Template | Example | Best for | Avoid when |
|---------|----------|---------|----------|------------|
| Number shock | "[number] [unit], [surprising comparison]" | "Seventy percent smaller. Barely dumber." | number-first, myth-bust | the number needs setup |
| Named contradiction | "[product] does [thing everyone thinks impossible]" | "This 120B model wakes up only 5B per word." | news-react, explainer | the claim is not in the brief |
| Situation | "You [do the thing]. Then [the wall]." | "You download the model. It will not fit." | smooth-explainer, worked-example | classic band (too slow) |
| Wrong diagnosis | "Your [X] isn't [Y]. Its [Z] is." | "Your GPU isn't slow. Its doorway is." | reframes, bandwidth, memory | the real cause needs two sentences |
| Decision | "[A] or [B]? One number decides." | "DGX Spark or RTX 5090? One number decides." | comparison-ladder | the brief has no deciding number |
| Price | "[amount] [per unit], [what you get]" | "Four thousand dollars. A hundred twenty-eight gigabytes." | hardware, cost stories | the price is the only fact |
| Case | "A [type of shop] [did the thing]. Here's what broke." | "A clinic pasted patient notes into a chatbot." | story-first, enterprise-privacy | no real case |
| Tonight | "Three moves and [the result] runs tonight." | "Three moves and DeepSeek runs on your box tonight." | how-to-three-moves | the moves need sudo and luck |

## Scoring the 10

Give each candidate one point for: the payoff word inside the first five words; a named product or a number; 5-12 words (classic) or 5-14 (smooth); legible as frame-1 text in 8 words or fewer; no hype word; true per the brief; and one more for naming the viewer's situation from the brief's `viewer_situation` ("You've got a 4090", "You downloaded it and it will not load").

Keep the **two** highest, one for each draft, and make them different kinds of hook: two Number-shock openers are one hook written twice. On a tie prefer the one a viewer could repeat to a friend.

The hook is never the wry beat. Wit lives in the middle of the script; the first sentence carries the promise and nothing else.

## Frame 1

The hook text is fully legible at frame 1 and something moves within 0.5 s. Digits on screen, words in narration.
