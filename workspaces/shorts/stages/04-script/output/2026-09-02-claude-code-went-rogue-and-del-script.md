# Claude Code went rogue and deleted years of work

## Decisions
- Structures tried: story-first (draft A) vs contrarian-take (draft B); A won 21 to 16 on the judge rubric. Both cleared rotation (last two: myth-bust, news-react-so-what).
- Hook: Case ("A Bengaluru heritage project let an AI agent work beside a decade of stone scans. Here's what broke.") over Situation; no hook graft (row 1: 2 vs 1).
- Graft taken: B's trade-off sentences appended to A's second step scene; the judge's reason: B prices the controls and A prescribed them cost-free.
- The first, second, third labels in the step scenes name viewer-performed actions (move, set permissions, test a restore) and stay inside the three-label budget for a has_process brief.
- Direct address arrives late (scene six onward) because the story opens on the case; the person advisory is accepted for a story-first shape.
- Unattended checkpoint calls: structures as above; hook pick 1 of 10; promise as in frontmatter.

## Hook candidates
1. A Bengaluru heritage project let an AI agent work beside a decade of stone scans. * (pick, Case, draft A)
2. You let a coding agent run shell commands where your real work lives. * (pick, Situation, draft B)
3. For four minutes, a man watched an AI delete his life's work. (Case)
4. An AI agent tried to stop itself. Its safety system said no. Twice. (Named contradiction)
5. Fifteen percent of a decade, gone in four minutes. (Number shock)
6. The agent was safe. The archive was not. (Named contradiction)
7. You trust the sandbox. The sandbox has cracks. (Wrong diagnosis)
8. Guardrails did not save this archive. Backups did. (Wrong diagnosis)
9. One stray quote mark deleted a heritage archive. (Case)
10. Keep the agent. Shrink its reach. (Decision)
