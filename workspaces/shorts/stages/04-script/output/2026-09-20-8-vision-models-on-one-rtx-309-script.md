# Eight vision models on one thirty ninety: the receipt

## Decisions

- Structures tried: contrarian-take (A) vs story-first (B). The hub pillar is myth-bust, but the
  ledger hard-bans it: the last two shipped scripts are myth-bust (2026-09-19) and number-first
  (2026-09-18), and their hook patterns (named-contradiction, number-shock) are equally banned.
  contrarian-take carries the same belief-vs-evidence spine without breaking rotation; A won the
  duel 23 to 15 and judge fairness row notes are recorded in the drafts note.
- Hook: wrong-diagnosis pattern, "Everyone misread this GPU's retirement". Product and tension
  land in the first five words of the narration; number-shock and named-contradiction were
  avoided on purpose because the last two scripts opened on them.
- Graft taken from B: "Memory from all of them, speed from the few." into the mixture-of-experts
  scene; it states the memory-versus-speed split A only implied, adds no numbers, and keeps A's
  person and structure.
- Value delivery: PROVES is the receipt beat, "One tester ran eight vision models on the card
  everyone calls obsolete" plus the one hundred thirty-nine point three tokens a second decode;
  TEACHES is the mixture-of-experts mechanism scene and the KV cache honest-catch.
- Gates: validate_storyboard 0 blockers, 2 advisories kept on purpose -- zero YouTube keyword
  tags (stage 05 owns the tags box), s03 est 12 s (one continuous routing-redraw visual; splitting
  it would break the single mechanism shot). eval_short exit 0, all nine gates pass,
  sameness clean against the last five. Normalizer: 2 scenes changed (spoken forms only).
- Payoff timing: first concrete fact ("One tester ran eight vision models") is sentence two of
  the hook scene, spoken by second 4 of the 40 s estimate.
- Ending: the last spoken sentence is the payoff ("For vision work, your card's limit is speed
  and patience, not capability."), zero tail sentences after it.

## Notes for review

tester" and owns the hedge in the honest-catch beat. The mixture-of-experts scene runs 12 s as
one continuous routing redraw; the advisory was kept because splitting it breaks the single
mechanism shot. The "card everyone calls obsolete" phrasing replaces the tester's r/LocalLLaMA
specifics; the used-price number was left out of narration (it is in the brief and available to
the package stage). RTX is spoken "R T X thirty ninety" per the lexicon.
