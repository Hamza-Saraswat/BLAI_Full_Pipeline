# RTX 3090 Still Runs Local AI: The One Spec That Decides What Fits

## Decisions
- Structures tried: number-first (draft A) and story-first (draft B); both clear rotation off the last two shipped shapes (worked-example, myth-bust). B won 21 to 20 with no grafts: it keeps the pairs-of-cards qualifier everywhere the basement job is cited, while A's number-shock hook ("One old GPU") implied one card ran a pairs job and was capped for drift.
- Hook: candidate 2 of 10, pattern case ("A basement. Pairs of 3090s."). Scored best on truth plus tension: it names the hardware and the surprise without the singular-card drift; the number-shock pick (candidate 1) lost for exactly that reason.
- Grafts: none. The judge found no loser line worth moving that would not break the winner's shape.
- Value lines: REFRAMES lands on "Obsolete meant not enough memory, and yours never had that problem."; EQUIPS lands on "One spec decides what fits" plus the VRAM definition in scene five.
- entity_spend soft-gate advisory kept: the script names RTX 3090, RTX 4090, vLLM, Gemma, LLaMA, NVLink, Trust Me Bro Reviews, Reddit, Hacker News; the extractor's remaining misses are spec-sheet fragments (GDDR7, Tensor Cores, BF16) the number budget excludes on purpose.
