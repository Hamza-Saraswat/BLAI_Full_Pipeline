## Winner and grafts

Winner: draft A, 21 to 19. Judge's reason: "B has the stronger hook and faster first concrete
beat, but its named-contradiction opening repeats the hook pattern of ledger script #4 and its
worked-example shape echoes #3, zeroing row 6, and its limit case is only asserted via the
closing aphorism while A shows Confident Mode Collapse concretely enough that a viewer could
predict novel failure cases. A's number-first shape, bare-number opening rhythm, and
aphoristic landing are all absent from the last five scripts, and its numbers are spent
exactly where the contrast lands hardest."

Grafts: none. (The loser's hook scored one point higher on row 1, under the two-point graft
threshold, and grafting it would have broken A's number-first opening law.)

What the losing shape would have needed: "B needed an opening rhythm not already in the
ledger: its isn't-lying-is-its-memory contradiction is the same hook move as the DeepSeek
script two runs back, which caps its difference score at zero regardless of execution quality.
It also needed to dramatize its own limit, for example a fourth card repeating one wrong
spark-plug answer in identical calm styling, so that agreement-is-evidence-not-truth is shown
rather than declared."
