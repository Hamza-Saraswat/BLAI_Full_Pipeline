## GMKtec EVO-X2 External Hardware Overview

First off, something that I wanted to point out is that we are going to show this system set horizontally, but it is actually supposed to be vertical.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Top](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Top-800x534.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-top/) GMKtec EVO X2 Top

The rubber feet are only at the bottom of the system. Still, we take horizontal photos, and the ports are labeled for horizontal reading, so that is how we are going to show the system.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Rubber Feet](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Rubber-Feet-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-rubber-feet/) GMKtec EVO X2 Rubber Feet

On the front, we get a power button that lights up. There is also a P-mode button (quiet, balanced, and performance modes) which perhaps some might use. We never used this other than to see what it did.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Front IO](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Front-IO-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-front-io/) GMKtec EVO X2 Front IO

Next to that we get a SD card reader, then a USB4 port. That is important for a mini PC to add expandability. The two other USB ports are Type-A USB 3.2 Gen2 ports.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Front IO Right](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Front-IO-Right-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-front-io-right/) GMKtec EVO X2 Front IO Right

There is also a headset jack on the front.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Front Angle 1](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Front-Angle-1-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-front-angle-1/) GMKtec EVO X2 Front Angle 1

On the rear, we get a lock point and a DC power input. There is also a headset jack. Networking is provided via a 2.5GbE port and a Realtek RTL8125BG. In a system at this price point, I wish this had 10GbE if not at least 5GbE.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Rear IO Left](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Rear-IO-Left-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-rear-io-left/) GMKtec EVO X2 Rear IO Left

Next to that we get a USB 3.2 Gen2 port, then another USB4 port. There are then DisplayPort and HDMI 2.1 ports. Next to those are the USB 2 ports.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Rear IO Right](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Rear-IO-Right-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-rear-io-right/) GMKtec EVO X2 Rear IO Right

The rear also has a lot of space dedicated to cooling. This is far from the old mini PCs that used 60-70W of yesteryear, so it needs space dedicated to cooling.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Rear Angle 2](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Rear-Angle-2-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-rear-angle-2/) GMKtec EVO X2 Rear Angle 2

Something that is probably underappreciated with this system is that it tapers off. Every time I see this top part, I think something along the lines of “I think I would rather that be an even edge.” To each your own, but for our readers sensitive to those things, it is very noticeable.

[![GMKtec EVO X2 Side 1](https://www.servethehome.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/GMKtec-EVO-X2-Side-1-800x533.jpg)](https://www.servethehome.com/gmktec-evo-x2-review-an-amd-ryzen-ai-max-395-powerhouse/gmktec-evo-x2-side-1/) GMKtec EVO X2 Side 1
