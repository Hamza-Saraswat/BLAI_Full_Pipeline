## GLOSSARY
GLOSSARY: Open-weight model | A model whose trained parameter files are published for anyone to download, run and fine-tune locally, without the training data or code needed to rebuild it from scratch.
GLOSSARY: Sovereign AI | A country's or organization's ability to run AI on infrastructure and models it controls, keeping data, customization and compute inside its own boundaries.
GLOSSARY: Series D | A company's fourth major named venture funding round, typically late-stage growth capital.
GLOSSARY: Post-money valuation | The company's worth immediately after the round closes, i.e. pre-money valuation plus the new cash.
GLOSSARY: Frontier model | A model at the measured capability edge of the field at a given time — what Mistral's headline says open-weight AI will become.
GLOSSARY: Full stack (Mistral's usage) | One vendor supplying the models, the infrastructure and compute they run on, and the production products, so customers are "never locked into a single vendor's roadmap."
