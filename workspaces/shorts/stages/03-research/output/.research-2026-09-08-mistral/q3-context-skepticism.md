## GLOSSARY: term | one-sentence definition

GLOSSARY: Sovereign AI | A country or company's ability to develop and deploy AI (models, data, compute) under its own jurisdiction without a foreign government or vendor being able to cut it off.
GLOSSARY: Open weights | A model whose trained parameter files you can download and run yourself, as opposed to API-only access — though the license may still restrict commercial production use.
GLOSSARY: Series A/B/C/D | Successive venture funding rounds; Mistral's ladder is EUR 385M (A, Dec 2023), EUR 600M (B, Jun 2024), EUR 1.7B (C, Sep 2025), and the reported EUR 3B (D, Sep 2026).
GLOSSARY: Post-money valuation | A company's worth immediately after a funding round closes, including the new cash.
GLOSSARY: BPI France | The French public investment bank and a recurring Mistral investor — the most direct state-linked money in the cap table.
GLOSSARY: ASML | Dutch semiconductor-equipment maker that led Mistral's EUR 1.7B Series C with EUR 1.3B for an ~11% stake, making Europe's chip-tool champion Mistral's largest shareholder.
GLOSSARY: GPAI (General-Purpose AI) | The EU AI Act category for foundation models; provider obligations applied Aug 2, 2025 and Commission enforcement powers switched on Aug 2, 2026.
GLOSSARY: Article 50 (EU AI Act) | The AI Act's transparency obligations for AI systems (e.g., disclosing when users interact with AI or deepfakes), applicable alongside GPAI enforcement from Aug 2, 2026.
GLOSSARY: Mythos | Anthropic's powerful cyber-capable model whose foreign access the U.S. Commerce Department temporarily cut off in June 2026 — the triggering event for Europe's sovereignty rush.
GLOSSARY: Annualized revenue run rate | Current-period revenue projected forward a year, used to compare fast-growing companies without waiting for full-year books.
GLOSSARY: MoE (Mixture of Experts) | An architecture that activates only a subset of the model's "expert" parameters per token, trading much larger total parameter counts for lower per-token compute. (Definition standard; no fetched page in this session assessed Mistral MoE sizing on consumer hardware — see UNVERIFIED.)
GLOSSARY: Export controls | US government restrictions that can deny foreign nationals or countries access to American models and chips — the mechanism behind the Mythos cutoff.
