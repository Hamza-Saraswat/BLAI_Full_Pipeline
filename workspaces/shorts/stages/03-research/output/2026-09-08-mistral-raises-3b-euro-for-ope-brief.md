## Glossary
- **open-weight model**: A model whose trained parameter files are published for download so you can run and fine-tune them yourself, without the training data or code needed to rebuild it from scratch.
- **Apache 2.0**: A permissive license granting royalty-free use, modification and distribution, including commercial, with attribution and notice retention.
- **MNPL (Mistral Non-Production License)**: Mistral's research-and-personal license: you may download, run and modify the model, but only in non-production environments and never supply it commercially without a separate grant.
- **mixture-of-experts (MoE)**: An architecture that stores many specialist sub-networks but routes each token through only a few, so total parameter count is huge while per-token compute stays small.
- **active parameters**: The subset of a mixture-of-experts model's weights that actually process a given token, for example 6.5B of Mistral Small 4's 119B.
- **sovereign AI**: A country's or organization's ability to run AI on infrastructure and models it controls, keeping data, customization and compute inside its own boundaries.
- **Series D**: A company's fourth major named venture funding round, typically late-stage growth capital.
- **post-money valuation**: The company's worth immediately after a funding round closes, meaning pre-money valuation plus the new cash.
- **GGUF**: The single-file quantized model format used by llama.cpp and LM Studio, the standard route for running models locally on consumer GPUs and Apple Silicon Macs.
- **NVFP4**: A 4-bit floating-point quantization format that shrinks checkpoints roughly four-fold versus BF16 for efficient serving on modern datacenter GPUs.
- **frontier model**: A model at the measured capability edge of the field at a given time.
