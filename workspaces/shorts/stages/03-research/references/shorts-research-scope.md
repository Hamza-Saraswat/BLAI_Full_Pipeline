# Shorts Research Scope

A Short spends one thesis and at most three spoken numbers. Research accordingly.

- Depth: standard (8-12 sources, 2-3 parallel questions). Go deep only when the pick is a correction of a published claim.
- The brief must carry: one thesis, 3-5 key numbers with units and sources, one analogy candidate with its limit, two misconceptions, a glossary of every term the narration will need to define, and the suggested outline as three beats.
- Primary sources first: release notes, model cards, official docs, benchmark pages, the creator's own measurements in `input/`. Community posts are leads to verify, not claims.
- Verbatim numbers: copy the figure and its unit exactly as printed; the writer rounds later, once.
- Contradictions: if two sources disagree, the brief says so and picks the one the channel can defend.
- Stop when the outline is supported; more sources do not make a better Short.
