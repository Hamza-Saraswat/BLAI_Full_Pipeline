# Outline Format

## Length

Target 10-14 minutes (hard band 8-20). Narration runs about 150 words per minute in this voice, so 1,500-2,100 words.

## Layout

```
---
slug: [slug]
series: [series]
structure: [structure]
value_types: A, B
target_minutes: 12
---

# Outline: [working title]

## Angle
One sentence a viewer could repeat.

## Value brief
- TEACHES: ...   (the specific thing)
- PROVES: ...    (the specific measurement)
- Hook: ...
- Payoff: ...
- The number by 0:20: ...

## Chapters
| # | Chapter | Target s | The one idea | Measurement shown | Muted viewer understands |
|---|---------|----------|--------------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | ... | 90 | ... | none | ... |

## Visual philosophy
Three sentences: the dominant scene types, the role of terminal captures, what stays typographic.

## Decisions
- Angle chosen and why; angles rejected.
```

## `[slug]-outlines.md`

Both competing outlines and the judge's work, in one note: outline A in full, outline B in full, then a score table with a row per dimension and a column per outline, the totals, the tiebreak when the totals tie, and one sentence of reasoning for every 0 or 1. Anything grafted from the loser into the winner is named here and in the winner's `## Decisions`.

## Chapter rules

- Chapter 1 carries the hook and the promise inside its first 30 seconds.
- Every chapter carries exactly one idea and ends on something usable.
- The last chapter delivers the payoff and never recaps the whole episode; a one-line "what to do tonight" is allowed.
- Chapter titles are 2-6 words and become the YouTube chapter labels.
- `structure` names the shape the outline was written under, from `episode-structures.md`. The script stage and the spec stage both read it.
