# Judge Rubric

Two outlines are written under two different structures from `episode-structures.md`. This rubric picks one. Score every dimension 0 to 3 for outline A and outline B, sum, and take the higher total (21 is the ceiling). A tie goes to the more different outline: the higher score on "Difference", then the structure the episode ledger has not seen longest.

Score the outlines, not the topic. Both carry the same facts, so every point comes from shape, placement and wording.

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook strength | needs setup, or reads the title aloud | one concrete thing, but buried in sentence two or three | concrete in sentence one, matches a pattern in the hook library | concrete in sentence one and a viewer would say "wait, what" |
| 2 | Promise inside 30 s | no promise, or an agenda list | a vague promise ("you will understand X") | one usable thing, named, but late in the first minute | one usable thing, named, and landed by 0:30 with the number by 0:20 |
| 3 | One idea per chapter | two chapters carry the same idea | one chapter carries two ideas | every chapter carries one idea, one is thin | every chapter carries one idea and ends on something usable |
| 4 | Payoff placement | payoff is in the last thirty seconds, or never arrives | payoff arrives but the episode re-argues it afterwards | payoff sits where the structure says, spent loosely | payoff sits where the structure says, and everything after it is consequence |
| 5 | Muted-viewer legibility | the visual philosophy is "talking head plus text" | some chapters name a visual, others do not | every chapter names what changes on screen | a muted viewer follows the one thing the structure says they follow, start to finish |
| 6 | Difference from the last five | same structure and same road as a recent episode | different structure, same road | different structure and a different road | different structure, different road, and a hook shape the last five do not use |
| 7 | Repeat test | no sentence a viewer would repeat | the payoff is repeatable but needs the video for context | the payoff is one repeatable sentence | the payoff is one repeatable sentence and the title could be built from it |

## Scoring notes

- Dimension 6 reads the last five entries of the episode ledger (`structure`, `series`, `slug`). A missing or empty ledger scores every outline 3 and says so.
- Dimension 2 is failed, not scored down, when the surprising number is absent from the outline: send that outline back rather than judging it.
- A dimension scored 0 or 1 gets one sentence of reasoning in the scores note. A 2 or 3 needs no defense.

## After the pick

The winner is saved as the outline the script stage reads. The loser is not thrown away: the judge may graft its hook line or one chapter idea into the winner, and says so in one line under `## Decisions`. Both outlines, all fourteen scores and the reasoning go to `output/[slug]-outlines.md` so the pick stays auditable.
