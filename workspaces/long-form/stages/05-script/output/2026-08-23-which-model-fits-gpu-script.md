# Which build fits the card you own

## Decisions
- Chapter order, count and labels carried unchanged from the outline. Chapter targets honoured: 105, 165, 135, 165 and 150 seconds, estimated at 150 words per minute.
- No first-party measurement exists, so there are no `[measured]` markers and every Capture cue cell is empty. Beat 1.2 says this out loud before any number is spoken, and every figure after it names its publisher.
- The `ollama ps` beat uses `code-typing`, not `terminal-replay`. A replayed capture would imply a run on our bench; the outline's visual philosophy asks for the shape of Ollama's documented output instead.
- One analogy in the whole episode: the desk, from `brand-vault/signature-analogies.md`, at beat 2.3, with its limit stated in the same beat. Fresh wording; no sentence is lifted from an earlier script.
- The single allowed "not X, but Y" is spent on the payoff's "bits, not parameters", as the outline instructs. Every other contrast is written as two positive sentences.
- Positional labels are absent. Chapter breaks navigate by content: 2 answers the question 1 raised, 3 jumps to the consequence, 4 names what changes, 5 contradicts the expectation.
- The graft recorded under the outline's `## Judge` lands at beat 3.4: the viewer's own parameters-times-bits sum is spoken, then contradicted by llama.cpp's measured bits per weight. No new source enters.
- Model names are spoken by publisher and parameter count rather than by repository string, because the voice engine mangles "Qwen3.8-27B" and the exact string belongs on screen anyway.
- Nothing follows the payoff. Beat 5.10 is the last spoken line of the episode.
