# Episode Signals

A Short answers one question; an episode walks one road. For each top item write one line that answers both:

1. Does it carry 10 minutes? Yes when there is a process with at least three visible steps, a comparison with at least three axes, or a concept with a worked example plus two edge cases. No when the whole point fits in one sentence (send it to the Shorts lane by noting "Short material").
2. What would we measure on the Spark? Name the command family (ollama, llama-server, vllm, a benchmark script) and the number (tokens per second, memory, load time, quality spot check). "Nothing to measure" is a valid answer and lowers the PROVES value.
3. Which series? One of the six in content-pillars.md. Input notes default to `my-dgx-spark-projects`.

Prefer items where the creator has already done the work (input lane) over items that would need a fresh experiment.
