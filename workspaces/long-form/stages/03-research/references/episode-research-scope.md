# Episode Research Scope

- Depth: deep (15-25 sources, 4-5 parallel questions): what it is, how it works, what it costs, what breaks, who else measured it.
- The brief must carry: the thesis; a chapter-shaped suggested outline (3-6 chapters); 6+ key numbers with sources; 2+ analogy candidates with limits; 3+ misconceptions; a glossary of every term the narration defines; the list of what we will measure ourselves.
- The creator's input note is a primary source: its numbers are `own-measurement`, its commands seed the experiment plan.
- The experiment plan is a contract with the capture stage: commands that will run unattended on the Spark at night. Plan for 30 minutes of wall time at most; prefer commands that print a number.
- Expected values: for each planned command write the number the sources predict; the capture stage compares.
- Stop at 25 sources. Beyond that, research time is better spent on the experiment plan.
