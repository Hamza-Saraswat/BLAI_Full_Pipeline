The radar's episode-signal line called it a changelog, and `selection-rules.md` says discard candidates that cannot carry ten minutes before sorting by opportunity. The rule order saved the run; the score on its own would have chosen it. This is the second live confirmation of the demand-signal problem recorded as finding 5.
- **Pick:** rank 2. It carries three contenders on one axis and ends in a rule a viewer can screenshot, it is evergreen rather than news, and the series ledger is empty so no rotation constraint applies.

- **Runner-up:** rank 4, the same question the top Shorts candidate asked. Held for a later episode so the two formats do not cover identical ground on the same day.
