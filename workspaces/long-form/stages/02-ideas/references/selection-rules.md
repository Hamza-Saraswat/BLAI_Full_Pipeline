# Selection Rules

## Cadence

3 episodes per week, produced on Mon/Wed/Fri mornings and published the next morning after approval. One pick per run.

## Ranking

1. Sort by `opportunity`; discard candidates that cannot carry 10 minutes (see the radar's episode-signal line).
2. Input-lane candidates (the creator's own project notes) get +15 before sorting: real work beats commentary.
3. Discard candidates that repeat a published episode's road (same product and same process) within 90 days.

## Rotation

No series twice in a row (`output/series-ledger.json`). Across any rolling six runs, every series that has a viable candidate appears at least once.

## Favored series

None favored; rotation only. (Change this line to favor a series for a month, for example while a Spark project is in progress.)

## Value tags

Exactly two of TEACHES, EQUIPS, REFRAMES, PROVES. An episode with a capture plan should carry PROVES.

## Empty days

If no candidate scores above 50, pick the best evergreen concept from `local-ai-for-dummies` or `inference-engineering-at-home` and say so in Decisions; evergreen explainers are the backbone of search traffic.
